Key Words for Vocabulary in Context

These terms are the language you need to talk about how you inferred a meaning.

contextthe words and ideas surrounding a word
context clueevidence that helps reveal a meaning
inferreach a likely meaning from evidence
inferencethe meaning or conclusion you infer
definitiona direct explanation of meaning
exemplificationexamples that clarify a category or idea
contrastan opposite or different idea used as a clue
signal worda word such as however or such as that points to a clue
provisionaltemporary; acceptable until more evidence is available
word familyrelated forms such as analyse, analysis, analytical
evidencethe exact phrase, example, number, or contrast supporting your guess
verifycheck your inferred meaning after you have made a guess
Useful answer frame: “I infer that _____ means _____ because _____ provides a definition / example / contrast / general-knowledge clue.”

A Reader's Strategy for an Unfamiliar Word

The handout's central advice is simple: do not stop immediately.

When You Meet a New Word

Keep reading to the end of the sentence.
Read the next sentence as well.
If the general meaning is still unclear, return to the unfamiliar word.
Look for a definition, example, contrast, or useful topic knowledge.
Make a provisional guess that fits the context.

Warm-Up — Do Not Use a Dictionary

Four Types of Context Clue

The same four clue types from the handout are applied here to engineering language.

1. Definition

The writer tells you what the unfamiliar word means.

A transient, or a short-duration change in voltage or current, may occur during switching.
Clue: the definition follows or.

2. Exemplification

Examples show what belongs to the meaning.

Nonlinear loads, such as rectifiers and variable-speed drives, can introduce harmonics.
Clue: examples follow such as.

3. Contrast

An opposite or different idea helps reveal the meaning.

Unlike a stable output, an oscillatory response repeatedly rises and falls.
Clue: unlike sets up the contrast.

4. General Knowledge

Your knowledge of the topic helps you interpret the word.

The heat sink reached 105 °C, and the technician stopped the test to prevent damage because the device had overheated.
Clue: the technical situation supports the meaning.
Mixed academic contexts

Context Clues Beyond Electrical Engineering

Vocabulary-in-context is a general academic reading skill. These examples deliberately come from different fields.

Public health — definition:
A disease is endemic, meaning that it is regularly present in a particular population or region.
Key words: meaning that · regularly present
Environmental science — exemplification:
Arid environments, such as deserts and very dry plateaus, receive little rainfall.
Key words: such as · deserts · dry
Economics — contrast:
Consumer demand was buoyant in the first quarter; however, it weakened considerably later in the year.
Key words: however · weakened later
Computing — numerical context:
The added processing delay was negligible: only 2 ms compared with a total response time of 480 ms.
Key words: only 2 ms · compared with 480 ms

Useful clue: endings such as -tion / -sion / -ity often mark nouns, -al / -ive often mark adjectives, and -ly often marks adverbs. These are tendencies, not perfect rules.

Activity C — Choose the Correct Family Member

  1. The controller improved voltage ________. (regulate)
  2. The output remained ________ after 0.3 s. (stability)
  3. The measured values ________ between 219 and 222 V. (variation)
  4. Harmonic ________ was performed using FFT. (analyse)
  5. The current was ________ 4.8 A. (approximation)
  6. Load current is a ________ that changes with operating condition. (vary)

Applied Mini-Reading: Infer Before You Look Up

Use the whole paragraph, not just the sentence containing the unfamiliar word.

During the inverter test, an intermittent switching fault appeared during only four of twenty start-up events and was absent during the remaining trials. The team added an LC filter to mitigate the output ripple; after installation, ripple decreased from 6.1% to 2.0%. The voltage deviation, or difference between the measured and reference voltage, remained below 1.5%. Unlike the earlier controller, which became unstable when the load changed rapidly, the revised controller was robust and maintained acceptable performance across all three tested loads. The steady-state current was approximately 5 A, with measured values ranging from 4.8 A to 5.2 A.
